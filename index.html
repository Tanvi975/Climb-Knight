Welcome to, Climb Knight


How to Play

Move: A / D or left/right arrow key

Climb: W / S or up/down arrow key

Jump: Space

Jump Left: J

Jump Right: K

Goal: Climb as high as possible

Collect: Coins to earn points

Avoid: Monsters

Lives: You have 3 lives
